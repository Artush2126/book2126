About the Book
This book begins with a direct statement: the author declares that he has succeeded in scientifically proving the existence of God. For more than 2500 years humanity has asked one question: Does God exist? A 1000-Day Journey to Test the Reality of God by Asking the Question: Does God Exist? This book does not ask you to believe. It does not try to convince. It presents a real life, a real search, and a real process that is happening now. The author describes his journey - from a personal question to a public challenge. A 1000-day open verification in which the hypothesis «The existence of God is scientifically provable» is placed into open examination. This is not a religious book. This is not a scientific textbook. This is not philosophy. This is a real attempt to bring one of humanity’s oldest questions into open testing. You are not only a reader. By reading this book, you become a witness to a process taking place in real time. This book follows the path of one person - from childhood, through work, success, loss, and search - to the moment where a question is placed in the open. If this process reaches its conclusion, this book may remain not only as something that was read, but as a witness to a moment in history. This book does not ask for faith. It asks only one thing: Stand where the question is being examined. Do not wait for history. Be present while it happens.
Author's Message
I wrote this book because the question was closed. And a closed question cannot be tested. This is not a book of faith. This is an invitation to test the question. If you are reading this book, you are already a participant.
